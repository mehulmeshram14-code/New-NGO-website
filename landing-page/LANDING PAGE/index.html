ABOUT DEEPROOTS FOUNDATION

Rooted in Vidarbha. Driven by Hope.

From Melghat to Gadchiroli, we work to bridge the gap between hunger and opportunity. Our mission is to ensure every individual has access to nutritious food, quality education, and a dignified future.

Vidarbha has historically faced deep structural challenges — from severe child malnutrition in the Satpura ranges of Melghat to agrarian distress. DeepRoots was founded to plant roots of resilience through direct, verifiable grassroots intervention.

PURPOSE & DIRECTION

Nourish Today. Empower Tomorrow.

We believe food is the foundation of human potential. We aim to fight hunger first, then create pathways to education, employment, and long-term community development.

OUR LONG-TERM VISION

FOUR PILLARS OF ACTION

Creating Change That Lasts.

Holistic, sustainable initiatives designed to address the root causes of underdevelopment.

01
🍲

Food Security & Nutrition

Delivering nutritious food and community nutrition support to vulnerable families, tribal children, and destitute seniors across rural Vidarbha.

  • Daily & seasonal meal distribution drives
  • Targeted micronutrient supplements for kids
  • Community kitchen infrastructure
02
📚

Education & Literacy

Helping children stay in school while creating learning opportunities for women and underserved communities.

  • School retention & mid-day nutrition tracking
  • Remedial learning for tribal children
  • Adult functional literacy circles
03
🌱

Livelihood Development

Building skills, supporting women’s self-help groups (SHGs), and promoting sustainable income opportunities.

  • Micro-enterprise training for rural women
  • Agro-allied value addition workshops
  • Youth vocational skill development
04
🤝

Community Upliftment

Supporting tribal communities, women, children, elderly people, and families facing severe economic hardship.

  • Elderly care shelter nutritional visits
  • Disaster & emergency crisis relief
  • Rights awareness & documentation aid
